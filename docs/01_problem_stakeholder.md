# 01. Problem + Stakeholder

## Problem

Loan underwriters spend a large share of each file manually re-keying and cross-checking applicant financial documents � pay stubs, bank statements, W-2s/tax forms � against the numbers stated on the loan application. This work is repetitive, error-prone, and delays the time to decision.

Specifically:
- Figures (income, assets, existing debts) must be manually transcribed from scanned/uploaded documents into the loan origination system (LOS) or an underwriting worksheet.
- Underwriters then manually cross-check those transcribed figures against the applicant's stated numbers, looking for inconsistencies.
- Missing or illegible documents require a manual follow-up request to the applicant, adding delay and rework.

This is not a one-time problem � it happens on every file, every day, across the underwriting team, making it a strong candidate for measurable improvement.

## Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role in the problem |
|---|---|
| Loan underwriters | Perform the manual extraction, cross-check, and decisioning |
| Loan processors | Intake documents, chase missing items, do first-pass completeness checks |
| Loan operations manager | Owns team throughput, cycle-time, and error-rate metrics |
| Applicants (indirect) | Experience the delay caused by document back-and-forth and rework |

## Why it matters

Manual document review is a direct driver of underwriting cycle time and a source of transcription error risk. Reducing time-per-file and error/rework rates would improve both team capacity and applicant experience without changing the underlying credit decision policy.
